About Sterling & Co. Properties

Our Story, Values, and People

Our History

Sterling & Co. Properties was established in 2011 in Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa. Founded by the Nene family with a vision to make property buying, selling, and renting transparent and stress-free, the agency began as a two-person office serving the Southern Suburbs.

Over the years, it expanded into a full-service brokerage with 34 agents, covering residential, commercial, and holiday-rental portfolios across the greater Cape Town metropolitan area. By 2019, Sterling & Co. Properties had earned multiple regional industry awards, including Top Independent Agency in the Western Cape.

The agency has undergone two major rebranding exercises — in 2016 and 2023 — to modernise its market positioning while preserving its reputation for meticulous, client-first service. It is a proud member of the Estate Agency Affairs Board (EAAB) and the Real Estate Business Owners of South Africa (REBOSA).
